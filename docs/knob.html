The knob

Every knob has three actions. Click the knob on the 3D pad to see them all in the side panel.

↺Turn Left
↻Turn Right
Press the knob for a third action. Mute / Unmute and Play / Pause toggle each time you press.

One-click presets

Presets set all three actions together, so they always match.
