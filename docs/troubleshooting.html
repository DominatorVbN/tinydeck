Troubleshooting

Most problems come down to the cable or the connection. Start there.

The status light on the on-screen pad stays dark until your pad is connected.

The pad isn't detected

Try a USB cable that carries data, plug straight into the Mac (no hub), and set any USB/Bluetooth switch to USB. Then unplug and replug.

Upload fails

Unplug the pad, plug it back in, and upload again. If it keeps failing, choose Help ▸ Report My Pad. The report includes the error.

A key does something different

Turn on Test Keys and press it. If TinyDeck shows another key, check Pad Info ▸ Orientation, or report it.

The knob turns the wrong way

Swap Turn Left and Turn Right, or apply a preset and upload again.

“This pad model is experimental”

TinyDeck can program it, but nobody has confirmed that model yet. It's safe to try, and you can always upload again. Please report whether it worked.

Still stuck? Open an issue.
