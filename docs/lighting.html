Lighting

Click the lightbulb in the toolbar. Choose an effect and, where your pad supports it, a colour. The pad changes instantly, and the 3D pad previews it.

Preview: Always On in purple.

Effects

Always On

Keys stay lit.

Light on Press

A key glows when you press it.

Shock

A flash ripples out on each press.

Off

No lights.

Colours

Larger pads (USB 1189:8840 family) offer eight colours, set separately for each layer.

1189:8890 pads accept an effect number only: Off, Always On and Effects 1–8. Try them while watching your pad.
