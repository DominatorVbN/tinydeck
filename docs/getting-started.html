Getting started

From unboxing to a pad that does exactly what you want. It takes about two minutes.

⌘C⌘V⌘Z▶︎🔇⏭
Your pad, mirrored in 3D. Keys light up as you press them.

Plug in your pad

Use a USB cable that carries data; many pads ship with charge-only cables. Plug it straight into your Mac. The light on the on-screen pad glows orange when it's connected. No pad yet? Choose Help ▸ Try Demo Pad.

Choose what each key does

Click a key on the 3D pad. In the side panel, pick Shortcut (click the field and press keys, like ⌘C), Media, Mouse or None. For a head start, use ⋯ ▸ Start From Preset.

Upload

When something changes, Upload Changes appears under the pad with a red dot. Click it, or press ⌘U. The pad stores your setup itself, so it works on any computer without TinyDeck running.

Check it

Turn on Test Keys in the toolbar, then press keys and turn the knob. The on-screen pad lights up and turns with the real one.
