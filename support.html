Frequently asked questions

Does TinyDeck need to keep running?

No. Click Upload and the pad stores your setup itself. It works on any Mac or PC afterwards, even without TinyDeck installed.

My pad isn't detected.

Many pads ship with a charge-only USB cable. Try a cable you know carries data, and plug straight into your Mac. If your pad has a USB/Bluetooth switch, set it to USB. See Troubleshooting.

Is my pad supported?

TinyDeck supports the CH57x family used by most “mini keyboard” macro pads. Check the compatibility list, or plug it in. TinyDeck tells you what it found.

Can a key mute and unmute?

Yes. Mute / Unmute and Play / Pause are toggles. In the knob editor, choose the Volume or Media preset.

Why can't I pick colours for my pad's lights?

Some pads (USB ID 1189:8890) only accept an effect number. Larger pads in the family have full colour and per-layer lighting.

I don't have a pad yet. Can I try it?

Yes. Choose Help ▸ Try Demo Pad. Everything works, but nothing is sent to hardware.

Can I use my ch57x-keyboard-tool YAML files?

Yes. File ▸ Import Mapping reads them, and File ▸ Export Mapping writes files the command-line tool accepts.

Help other pad owners

Whether your pad works or not, choose Help ▸ Report My Pad in TinyDeck. It fills in your pad's details for you.
