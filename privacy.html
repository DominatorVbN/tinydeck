Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Applies to TinyDeck for Mac

The short version

TinyDeck doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your pads, key mappings and settings are saved in TinyDeck's own sandboxed storage on your Mac. They never leave your Mac unless you export them yourself.

USB access

TinyDeck uses macOS's USB permission only to detect supported macro pads and to write your configuration to them. It doesn't read or record anything else from your USB devices.

Key testing

When you turn on Test Keys, TinyDeck shows the key presses it receives while its own window is active, so you can check your pad. These presses aren't stored or sent anywhere. TinyDeck can't see what you type in other apps.

Pad reports

If you choose Help ▸ Report My Pad, TinyDeck opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub in your browser. It contains your pad's details and current mapping, which are shown before you submit. Nothing is sent unless you submit the issue yourself, and GitHub's own privacy policy applies to anything you post there.

Children

TinyDeck collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
